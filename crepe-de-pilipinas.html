04 / About

Made by students,
folded to order.

Crepe De Pilipinas is a student booth run by a group of seven Grade 12-ICT students at La Salle College Antipolo. We make Filipino-inspired crepes in six flavors: original, sweet, and made with love.

Find us at the campus booth during breaks, lunch, and after class. You can also find us in the 12 I classroom.
